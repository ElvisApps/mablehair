Our Story

Authentic African beauty traditions, bottled for you.

Mable Hair Care was born out of a profound respect for natural African hair-care traditions. For centuries, women in Chad and across the continent have relied on the earth's natural bounties, like Chebe powder and Karkar oil, to maintain strong, resilient, and beautiful hair.

We saw the need to make these authentic ingredients accessible to everyone seeking a simpler, more effective natural hair routine. Our products are formulated to moisturize, strengthen, and support length retention naturally, without relying on harsh chemicals or artificial additives.

At Mable, we believe that healthy-looking hair starts with consistent care and natural ingredients. We are committed to sourcing ethically, formulating thoughtfully, and celebrating the unique beauty of natural textured hair.

100% Natural

We harness the power of botanicals, essential oils, and traditional herbs to care for your hair gently and effectively.

African Heritage

Our formulations are heavily inspired by ancient Chadian and Sudanese hair care secrets passed down through generations.
